Mission: Solve the Tech Heist Murder

Scenario: In a high-tech lab, a billionaire tech mogul named Elias was found dead after a heist gone wrong. Three suspects were seen fleeing: Sam (business partner, hacker), Garfield (rival inventor, engineer), Andrew (assistant, spy).

Three possible weapons: Laser Gun, Poison Dart, Cyber Implant.

Three possible motives: Rivalry, Betrayal, Greed.

Clues:

  1. The murderer is not the one who had a public rivalry with Elias; that person was Garfield, who argued with him openly.
  2. Sam, the hacker, would never use a physical weapon like the laser gun—he prefers digital methods.
  3. The motive wasn't greed, as the stolen data was worthless to outsiders.
  4. Andrew, the spy, betrayed Elias by leaking secrets, but only after the murder.
  5. If Garfield used the cyber implant, then the motive was rivalry; but Garfield denies it.
  6. The poison dart was found in Sam's pocket, but he claims it was planted.
  7. No one with a motive of betrayal would use the laser gun, as it's too traceable.
  8. Andrew had access to the cyber implant, but he swears he didn't touch it.
  9. The murderer used the cyber implant, which requires engineering skills that only Garfield has.
  10. Sam had a motive of greed for the data, but the data was encrypted and useless without Elias's key.
